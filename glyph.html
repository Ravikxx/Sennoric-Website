Glyph

Our small, fast model. Formerly Veil, now renamed and running on its own dedicated endpoint.

Live now, free with a Sennoric account.

What Glyph is

Glyph 1.1 is a fine-tuned Llama 3.1 3B: small, fast, and free with a Sennoric account. It was our first model, built before Fresco existed, and everything we learned fine-tuning and shipping it shaped how we built Fresco 1.2.5.

Where it fits

Fresco is the flagship, built for general chat, writing, and code. Glyph is smaller and faster, built for quick, lightweight conversations where a 3B model is plenty. It stays in the lineup for now; a third tier, Mosaic, is coming soon and will eventually take its place.

What changed, and when

  • Jul 25, 2026Marked deprecated and briefly taken offline while its compute was committed to the Project Crucible 600M rerun.
  • Jul 30, 2026Back online and answering requests as normal.
  • Aug 2026Moved onto a dedicated RunPod Serverless endpoint behind our Cloudflare Worker, its own infrastructure rather than a shared Hugging Face Space.
  • Aug 6, 2026Renamed from Veil to Glyph as part of the move from Sennoric to Sennoric. No longer deprecated, and no retirement date set. It'll be replaced eventually, likely once Mosaic ships, but stays up for now.

Using it

Same OpenAI-compatible endpoint as always: model: "veil", same free Sennoric account. The wire model id stays veil even though it's called Glyph everywhere you read it.
